Welcome to Price Check

A one-click button that asks your own AI assistant to find a better price for the product you're looking at. Works with Grok Bot webhook routines.

✓ You're set up. Open any product page and click the Price Check button in your toolbar.

What it does

When you're on a product page in any online store, click Check price. The extension sends the product's name, price, store and product codes to your Grok Bot. Your Grok Bot then looks for the same item at other stores and posts the results in your Grok Bot chat. Full check also covers price history, reviews, return policies, shipping and coupons, and takes a few minutes.

The extension can't show results by itself. They always appear in your Grok Bot chat.

What you need

Your own AI assistant that can receive webhooks, such as a Grok Bot. The extension doesn't do the price search itself. It hands the page to your assistant, so you set it up once below. The steps below are written for Grok Bot. Takes about 2 minutes.

Set it up in 3 steps

  1. Copy the setup prompt and paste it into your Grok Bot chat

    It asks your Grok Bot to create a webhook routine named Check price from browser extension.

  2. Copy the webhook URL and key into Options

    When your Grok Bot has created the routine, it gives you a webhook URL and a sender key. Paste both into the extension's Options. If your bot says the key goes in a custom header or a query parameter, pick that under Send key as; otherwise leave it on Authorization: Bearer.

  3. Save, then Send test

    Click Save and allow Chrome to contact your webhook's site (only that site). Then click Send test. Your Grok Bot should reply in chat that the connection works.

Tips

  • Pin the extension: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Price Check.
  • Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+P for a quick check, Alt+Shift+F for a full check. You can also right-click any page.
  • Privacy: the page is read only when you ask. Only product details, the page address and title go to your webhook. No cookies, form data or full page contents are ever sent, and the developer collects nothing. Privacy policy
